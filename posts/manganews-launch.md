# Launching MangaNews: anime & manga news + release calendar

<sub>📅 Posted on LinkedIn · 2026-11-01 · by **Ali Asad**, Director @ Techwest Solutions Ltd</sub>

<p align="center"><img src="images/manganews-launch.jpg" alt="Launching MangaNews: anime & manga news + release calendar" width="600"></p>

Every anime fan knows the loop: one site for news, another for release dates, a Discord server for countdowns, and you still miss the episode. So we built MangaNews.  

It puts anime and manga news in one feed and adds a release calendar with live countdowns for new episodes, premieres and movies. Follow a series and get notified before the next episode or chapter drops.  

A few things we cared about:  
- "More Coverage" groups the same story from several outlets, so you see every angle without duplicates  
- A release calendar with live countdowns, plus manhwa and manhua  
- 15 languages, picked automatically from your phone  
- No account required, no data collected  
- Links to official sources only. We never host scans or episodes  

It is live on Google Play and still improving. If you try it, tell me what is missing.
